Outputs Gallery
===============

Processed Analysis Results by Year
-----------------------------------

This section presents the spatially distributed analysis outputs generated by the processing pipeline, organized chronologically from 1990 baseline through 2024 final state. All visualizations are in PNG format at 30-meter pixel resolution, suitable for web display and document embedding.

Year 1990 Baseline
------------------

True Color Composite (RGB)

The true-color composite combines red, green, and blue reflectance bands to produce an image resembling natural color aerial photography. This visualization enables visual assessment of landscape characteristics and recognition of major land cover categories.

![1990 True Color Composite](../assets/outputs/1990/01_RGB.png)

Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI)

NDVI visualizes vegetation vigor and extent. Dark colors indicate low vegetation (water, bare soil, urban); bright colors indicate dense vegetation (forests, croplands). The color scale ranges from dark blue (NDVI = -1.0, non-vegetated) through yellow (NDVI = 0.0, neutral) to bright green (NDVI = 1.0, dense vegetation).

![1990 NDVI](../assets/outputs/1990/02_NDVI.png)

Normalized Difference Built-up Index (NDBI)

NDBI emphasizes urban and built-up areas. Dark colors indicate non-urban land cover (vegetation, water); bright colors indicate urban infrastructure (buildings, roads, parking). The visualization reveals the spatial pattern of urban development in 1990.

![1990 NDBI](../assets/outputs/1990/03_NDBI.png)

Normalized Difference Water Index (NDWI)

NDWI identifies water bodies and moisture-rich areas. Dark colors indicate dry conditions (bare soil, urban); bright colors indicate high moisture (water, irrigated crops, wet vegetation). The visualization reveals irrigation patterns and water body locations.

![1990 NDWI](../assets/outputs/1990/04_NDWI.png)

Land Use and Land Cover Classification

The supervised SVM classification assigns each pixel to one of five classes: Water (blue), Urban (gray), Agriculture (yellow-green), Forest (dark green), or Bare Soil (tan). The classification reveals the spatial organization of land cover types in baseline conditions.

![1990 LULC Classification](../assets/outputs/1990/05_LULC.png)

Multi-Temporal Change Results
------------------------------

Change Map 1990 to 2024

This map synthesizes post-classification comparison and index differencing results to show where land cover changed between 1990 and 2024. Changed areas are colored to indicate the nature of transition; stable areas are shown in gray.

![Change Map 1990 to 2024](../assets/outputs/change_1990_2024/change_map.png)

NDVI Change 1990 to 2024

This visualization shows the magnitude and direction of NDVI change. Bright colors indicate vegetation gain (regreening); dark colors indicate vegetation loss (greening to non-vegetated). Areas with no detectable NDVI change are shown in neutral gray.

![NDVI Change 1990 to 2024](../assets/outputs/change_1990_2024/ndvi_change.png)

NDBI Change 1990 to 2024

This visualization shows changes in built-up index, revealing where urban expansion occurred (bright areas of NDBI increase) and where urban areas may have decreased or converted to other uses (dark areas of NDBI decrease).

![NDBI Change 1990 to 2024](../assets/outputs/change_1990_2024/ndbi_change.png)

Classification Transition Map

This map encodes the post-classification comparison results as specific class-to-class transitions. Each color represents a distinct transition (e.g., Agriculture-to-Urban, Forest-to-Bare Soil). Unchanged pixels are shown in neutral colors. This visualization enables direct identification of dominant change processes.

![Classification Transition Map](../assets/outputs/change_1990_2024/transition_map.png)

Using These Visualizations
--------------------------

These PNG outputs are intended for inclusion in presentations, reports, publications, and policy briefings. All images are georeferenced (contain embedded spatial reference information) and can be incorporated into geographic information systems for further analysis.

Image specifications:

Format: PNG (lossless compression)
Color Model: RGB or indexed color
Resolution: 30 meters per pixel
Spatial Reference: UTM Zone 39 North, WGS84
Dimensions: Approximately 400 by 450 pixels
File Size: 2 to 10 MB per image

Citations should include the complete reference to this repository and the analysis date. Attribution to Landsat data sources (USGS) is recommended.
